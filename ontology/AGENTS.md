## Information locations

Paths are relative to the repository root.

- `ontology/domain/index.md` — effective domain, relations, constraints and generators.
- `ontology/instances/` — current enumerable instance data and design values.
- `ontology/decisions/AGENTS.md` — guidance for standalone decision records in `ontology/decisions/`.
- `docs/findings/todo_dispatch_in_files.md` — historical findings snapshot dispatch queue.
- `docs/research/` — relocated research documents.
- `docs/findings/sleep-clock-evidence.md` — historical sleep and clock evidence.
- `docs/findings/spirit-bell-evidence.md` — Spirit Bell evidence and qualified duration disagreement.
- `docs/ROADMAP/todo_decide.md` — unresolved game choices only; absent when none remain.
- `docs/todo_handle_reconciled_items.md` — approved selections awaiting closure; absent when none remain.
- `docs/ROADMAP/todo_implement.md` — deferred implementation, not undecided design.
- `ontology/README.md` — current overview, catalog and qualifications.

## Recording rule

Record significant decisions once in `ontology/decisions/` and source evidence once in
`docs/findings/`. Do not double-track them in `todo_decide.md`, including completed-item
indexes, approvals/refusals, application history, reviews, commands, logs, commits or
publication/execution narratives. Routine receipts belong in managed evidence, not the
pending-decision backlog. Delete `todo_decide.md` when no unresolved game choice remains.
