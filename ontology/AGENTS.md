- `ontology/domain/index.dsl` — effective domain, relations, constraints and generators.

- `docs/research/` — relocated research documents.

- `docs/findings/spirit-bell-evidence.md` — Spirit Bell evidence and qualified duration disagreement.
- `docs/ROADMAP/todo_decide.md` — unresolved game choices only; absent when none remain.
- `docs/todo_handle_reconciled_items.md` — approved selections awaiting closure; absent when none remain.
- `docs/ROADMAP/todo_implement.md` — deferred implementation, not undecided design.
- `ontology/README.md` — current overview, catalog and qualifications.
