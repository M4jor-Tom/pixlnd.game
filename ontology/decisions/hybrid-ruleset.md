# D1 — Hybrid ruleset

Use `ruleset-hybrid`: Alpha progression plus approved Steam content. Alpha/Steam rulesets
are historical comparisons, not additional playable modes. [Cut content](cut-content-roadmap.md),
[Omega scope](omega-roadmap.md) and [regional-loss exclusion](regional-gear-loss-exclusion.md) apply.

Reconciliation item 8 clarifies reference versus hybrid scope: D1–D26 and later approvals
stand; current-slice approximations are not final rules. It grants no new content inclusion,
exclusion or unresolved merge. Selecting hybrid rules does not settle historical source truth.
