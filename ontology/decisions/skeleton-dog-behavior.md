# Creature families items 7–8 — Skeleton Dog is a normal tameable dog

Skeleton Dog is **non-aggressive and tameable**, using the ordinary dog's encounter/state
baseline, not unconditional passivity. Ordinary aggression/retaliation, pack response and
taming reactions apply; once tamed, normal pet behavior applies. There is no Skeleton-Dog-only
retaliation or taming-provocation exemption.

“Skeletal” is a dog breed, not a playable race. Preserve general taming eligibility and settlement
protection; grant no riding permission, source ID, combat/loot bonus or trait inheritance.
[Taxonomy](skeleton-dog-primary-family.md) and [shared bait](dog-bubble-gum.md) remain distinct choices.
