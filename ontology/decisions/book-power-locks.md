# Books/formulas item 3 — Immediate learning, power-locked crafting

Books record recipes immediately, but above-power recipes remain **visibly locked against crafting**
until the power requirement is reached. Formula learning keeps its existing power requirement.
Known-but-locked counts as known for [duplicates](recipe-duplicates.md): no compensation, extra
learning, consumed known formula or crafting-gate bypass. Knowledge is not crafting access.
Canonical [power gate](../domain/3_Classes.md#power-gate-a).
