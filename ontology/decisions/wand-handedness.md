# Wand item 1 — Mechanically two-handed

A Wand is **mechanically two-handed**, despite a one-hand pose: equip **no other hand item**.
Existing damage and attacks and the **32-cube upgrade limit** stand. Under [D6](numeric-design.md),
the common recipe costs **20 wood cubes**; no new damage, pairing or upgrade exception follows.
Canonical [weapon-type](../domain/3_Classes.md#weapon-type).
