# Books/formulas item 2 — One recipe collection, no duplicate reward

Books and formulas teach into one character recipe collection. Books teach only unknown recipes,
with **no rerolls or compensation**; an already-known formula remains **unconsumed**.
Overlap can make later books less rewarding. No recipe-generation or identity default is selected.
[Known-but-locked recipes](book-power-locks.md) count as known, not as another learning reward.
Canonical [recipe](../domain/3_Classes.md#recipe).
