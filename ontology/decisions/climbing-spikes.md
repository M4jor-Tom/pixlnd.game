# Traversal item 4 — Climbing and Spikes

Basic climbing needs neither points nor Spikes. Climbing points reduce stamina drain;
globally acquired Spikes reduce consumption by **75%**, not infinite endurance.
Five Climbing points remain the [gliding prerequisite](gliding-gates.md).
[Remaining-cost stacking](climbing-cost-stacking.md) preserves points' benefit;
no skill-reduction curve/floor or artifact-combination rule is inferred.
