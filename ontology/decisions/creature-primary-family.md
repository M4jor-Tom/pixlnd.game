# Primary versus descriptive creature-family membership

A creature has at most one primary balancing family and may have multiple descriptive
memberships. Descriptive memberships never supply or stack family stat modifiers; family
membership grants no automatic species-trait inheritance or new numerical modifier.

Taxonomy alone changes no behavior, habitat, taming, drop or pack-aggro rule.
[Primary assignments](creature-family-assignments.md) and the
[Skeleton Dog choice](skeleton-dog-primary-family.md) are recorded separately.
