# Creature families item 7 — Collie supplies ordinary dog outcomes

Use Collie (`collie`) for ordinary outcomes in already-established skeleton-only dog encounters,
including the deadlands roster. Preserve other ordinary-dog selections, dog frequency and pack
sizes, and Collie's **untameable dungeon-patrol exception**. The [individual rarity roll](skeleton-dog-individual-rolls.md)
still applies; this grants no unrestricted Skeleton Dog spawns or new dog locations.

Item 7's tamability amendment, clarified by item 8, follows [normal dog behavior](skeleton-dog-behavior.md).
