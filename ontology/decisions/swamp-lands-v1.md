# Swamp Lands — hybrid v1 scope

Status: accepted. Owner decision: 2026-10-16. Exact answer: **“1: Approved”**.

Wetlands stays playable; **do not add a separate Swamp Lands biome to hybrid v1**.
Keep the uncertain historical reference without claiming the two were identical. This is
neither an alias declaration nor a permanent exclusion from every version.

Context: historical Swamp Lands identity is unverified; launch availability needs a rule
independent of that research. The choice avoids inventing a second biome's terrain, fauna
or rewards, at the cost of no separate Swamp Lands destination in v1. The alternative was
to add that distinct destination; no such content rules were approved. Further owner
reasoning was not supplied.

[climate generation](../domain/6_Generators.md#6-generators).
