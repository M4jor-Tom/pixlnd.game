# Creature families item 4 — Dog-relative Skeleton Dog rarity

Skeleton Dog has a **1% chance among dog spawns**, not among all creatures and not a guaranteed
quota. This is not a one-tenth relative species weight and grants no combat or loot bonus.
[Individual rolls](skeleton-dog-individual-rolls.md), [locations](skeleton-dog-locations.md)
and the [ordinary replacement pool](ordinary-dog-replacement.md) remain separate choices.
