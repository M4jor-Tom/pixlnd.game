# Free recovery is separate from paid inn sleep

Inn recovery fully heals and sets the respawn point **for free**; paid sleep is a separate service.
The existing hours, price and clock-skip destination remain in [game-clock](../domain/3_Classes.md#game-clock),
not new values inferred from historical sleep reports. [Ordinary midnight resets](inn-midnight-resets.md)
and [connected-player consent/payment](inn-sleep-consent.md) govern the skip.
