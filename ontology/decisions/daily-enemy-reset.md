# World/reset item 3 — Daily enemy eligibility

Eligible daily enemies return, including cleared ordinary dungeon and repeatable-encounter
enemies and bosses. Completed one-time objectives stay cleared. Resets never renew permanent
artifact/book claims or completed one-time objectives.

Exact eligibility and personal/shared ownership remain in [game-clock](../domain/3_Classes.md#game-clock)
and [save-data](../domain/3_Classes.md#save-data); other mechanics' reset/reward rules are unchanged.
[Occupied-site timing](occupied-site-refresh.md) applies. Existing
[arena encounters](../domain/3_Classes.md#arena-s) and [world-shared improvements](../domain/3_Classes.md#save-data)
keep their canonical rules; source encounter reports do not replace them.
