# Defer occupied-site daily refresh

An occupied dungeon/quest site waits for **all players to leave**. Multiple missed midnights
coalesce into **one pending refresh**, not stacked waves. The clock never heals or replaces
surviving enemies or erases an ongoing fight. Apply [daily eligibility](daily-enemy-reset.md),
not a new reward/reset rule; ordinary threat and home-return rules stand.
Canonical [game-clock](../domain/3_Classes.md#game-clock).
