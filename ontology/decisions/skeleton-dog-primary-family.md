# Creature families item 1 — Skeleton Dog belongs to dogs

Skeleton Dog (`skeleton-dog`) is primary **dogs**, not skeletons; descriptive dogs/skeletons
and its independent **undead** category remain. This creates no singleton family or
species-trait inheritance; [primary versus descriptive membership](creature-primary-family.md) applies.
[Normal dog behavior](skeleton-dog-behavior.md) is a separate decision.
