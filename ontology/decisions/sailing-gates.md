# Traversal item 3 — Sailing gates

Require **5 Swimming points**, **at least 1 Sailing point** and a **vendor-bought, equipped Boat**.
Further points improve speed; buying alone is insufficient. Boat and [Hang Glider](gliding-gates.md)
use the single special slot, never both simultaneously.
Canonical [skill tree](../domain/3_Classes.md#skill-tree-a).
