## When to Use
A fixed-budget schema test times out within bind_source before inverse/native-positive/case labels. Preserve the full test context. Owner prefers measuring with a generous timeout and calibrating limits rather than narrowing coverage to meet an old deadline.

## Procedure
1. Preserve original failed receipts, exact source/fixture/interpreter pins, full method/case inventory and actual phase reachability. Inspect tests/ontology_source_contracts.py class_contract/assert_declared_root and tests/test_ontology_schema.py generated before attributing the cost.
2. For a budget-only overrun, keep the entire test/helper/schema unchanged. Obtain or use explicit owner authority for one generous finite measurement budget, with ownership wait and Bash/subagent limits larger than the native limit; do not let the old wrapper deadline defeat the new measurement.
3. After a successful complete run, apply the repository formula from docs/quality-checks.md: normal native deadline = 3 × ceil(1.25 × measured duration). For the owner's requested total-runtime calibration, use the actual whole invocation receipt duration conservatively, disclose its boundary, preserve the generous first-run budget separately and persist the new normal native/wait/caller policy. Do not replay just to tidy reporting.
4. If source attribution is separately needed and explicitly approved, back up complete helper bytes/mode and protected input hashes, then prepare minimal synchronous monotonic/flush observations around source-view lookup, induced slots, independently requested generated(owner), check_schema and final root assertions.
5. Mechanically prove that removing only added observation statements yields the exact original whole-module AST; review simplify then ponytail. Install restoration cleanup before temporary source replacement. Genuine preflight must pin actual instrumented bytes while all other protected inputs match.
6. Run only the approved selector. Keep completed generation/check_schema wall boundaries distinct from CPU profiling; unfinished BEGIN labels prove no completed duration. Capture nonzero explicitly after successful preflight so cleanup runs.
7. After any temporary observation, restore helper byte/mode exactly, verify cmp/SHA and all protected baseline hashes without native replay, and return at the agreed barrier. No automatic optimization or additional native campaign follows.

## Pitfalls
- Owner explicitly refused narrowing the full required-weapon test to a movesets-only fixture/root to meet a timeout. Do not propose coverage/context reductions, root rewrites, case deletion or guard weakening as a deadline remedy.
- Independent generated(owner) and metaschema checking can each take seconds per cold root despite per-owner caching. Splitting cases does not remove shared setup.
- Do not interpret measured wall durations as exclusive CPU percentages, contention proof or a universal future runtime.
- The formula is 3*ceil(1.25*T), not ceil(3.75*T); record whether T excludes or includes Nix/setup/ownership bookkeeping.
- An instrumented pass/timeout belongs to its actual pins; restoration does not make it an uninstrumented pass.
- On the affected pinned CPython build, asynchronous faulthandler sampling faulted in its own dump thread; never blindly repeat that observer.

## Verification
1. All original full-source controls, cases, assertions and independently requested native-root guards remain intact.
2. The measurement completed successfully under its honestly recorded generous budget; a timeout or failing test is not a passing calibration.
3. Normal deadline and ownership/caller margins are recorded from the actual duration and exact repository formula, with old failed receipts preserved.
4. If temporary logging was used, whole-module AST equivalence and byte/mode/all-input restoration were independently verified; no timing code remains.
