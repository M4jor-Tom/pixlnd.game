# Weapons now fight their own way — 2026-09-11

Every weapon has its own attack (left click) and special (right click, hold to charge).

## Ranged weapons
- **Bow**: arrows fly where you aim and drop with distance. Special: a volley of four arrows that
  blast a small area.
- **Crossbow**: fast, flat bolts. Special: one heavy bolt.
- **Boomerang**: flies out, hits everything on its path several times, and comes back to you.
  Special: two boomerangs.
- **Staff**: a burst where your aim lands, up to twenty blocks away. Special: a bigger burst.
- **Wand**: an instant beam. Special: a thicker, stronger beam.
- **Bracelet**: quick bolts that never drop. Special: a ball that explodes and knocks enemies down.

## Melee weapons
- **Sword, axe, mace**: special spins and hits everything around you.
- **Greatsword, greataxe, greatmace**: slower, wider swings; every third hit can knock down.
  Special: a heavy blow that knocks down.
- **Dagger**: fast stabs. Special: an ambush that stuns and poisons.
- **Fist**: the fastest punches. Special: a kick that knocks down.
- **Longsword**: special lunges four blocks forward, hits hard and stuns.

## Class skills
- Fire Missiles throws four exploding fireballs, Bubbles throws six bursting bubbles, and
  Shuriken Attack throws five shuriken before the backflip.

## Combat
- Your combo counts each attack once, however many shots it fires, and only breaks when a whole
  attack misses.

## Also
- Start the game with `--class=ranger`, `mage` or `rogue` to try a bow, a staff or a dagger.
