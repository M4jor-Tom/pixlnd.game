# Ruins, rescues and returning battles — 2026-10-25

[Watch the demonstration](20261025043910_initial-encounters_6730708.avi)

## Explore and fight
- Find three-room ruins, a daily skeleton camp and four supplier rescues around each land's village, with connecting paths and boardwalks over water.
- Watch bosses warn before stomping or charging. Their specials smash stone rubble; the rubble returns after three world-days when it is safe.
- Avoid warning spikes, smash vases for copper and collect the flask and life potion waiting on each room's table. Breaking the table won't create a second pair.

## Collect and rescue
- Every boss drops shared gear. Ruins bosses also drop spirit cubes you can collect and carry.
- Clear the camp for a shared package of gear, copper, a life potion and gems.
- Free a supplier by defeating every guard, then talking to the captive or leaving the cleared site. Each rescue improves what the land's shops may offer at their next daily restock.

## Return to your world
- Shops remember their stock and your purchases when reopened or after saving and returning. Rescues don't reroll today's stock.
- Daily encounters wait until everyone leaves before renewing. Surviving enemies keep their state; completed supplier rescues stay cleared.
- Leave either hand empty independently: removing your sword can leave your shield equipped.
