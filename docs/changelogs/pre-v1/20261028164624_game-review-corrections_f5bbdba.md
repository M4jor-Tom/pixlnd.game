# Spinning strikes and useful Apples — 2026-10-28

[Watch the demonstration](20261028164624_game-review-corrections_f5bbdba.avi)

## Cyclone
- Sweep around enemies clockwise instead of striking everyone at once. Running out of stamina stops you where you are facing.
- Keep control of your camera while spinning; normal movement and your next attack still follow it.

## Items and inventory
- Apples now restore health when used, including from your quick item shortcut.
- Loot, rewards and shop items now use the correct item power for their source level. Newly equipped gear must still fit your character's power.
