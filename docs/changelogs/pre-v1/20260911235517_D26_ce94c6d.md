# Enemies shoot back — 2026-09-11

## Enemy types
- Humanoid bands (skeletons, gnobolds, nomads, mermaids, vampires…) now mix fighters, archers and mages; every band rolls its own mix.
- Fighters close in and bite, just like before.
- Archers keep their distance and loose arcing arrows at you.
- Mages hold back and lob slow fire bolts that burst on impact.
- Spitters spit poison that keeps ticking even if you dodge out of the way.
- Snout beetles charge heavy shots that pack a punch.

## Taking fire
- Enemy shots work like melee hits: dodge them, block them, or take the damage and show a hurt number.
- Getting hit by a shot can stun you or knock you back.
- Fire bolts leave you burning for a few seconds, taking damage each tick.
- Poison ticks through your dodge window—the toxin sticks even if you roll away.

## Line of sight
- Archers, mages, and other shooters need to see you to fire.
- Duck behind a wall and shooters stop; they back off if you close the distance on an archer.

## Other creatures
- Enemy shots never damage other creatures, only you.
