# Equipment that fits — 2026-10-21

## Armor and rings
- Gear meant for another class appears red in your bag and cannot be equipped. Armor made from your class's material equips normally.
- You can wear two separate rings, one in each ring slot. An item you wear no longer appears as a second copy in your bag.

## Weapons and hands
- A second one-handed weapon can go in a legal off-hand slot. A two-handed weapon cannot replace your main-hand weapon while your off-hand is occupied.
- Mages can equip a Wand when the off-hand is clear; with two bracelets equipped, trying to equip the Wand leaves both in place.
