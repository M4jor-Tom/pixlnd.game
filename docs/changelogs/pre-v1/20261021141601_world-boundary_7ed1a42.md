# World boundary — 21 October 2026

## Exploring
- Stop at the world's edge and walk back inward without taking edge damage.
- Teleport stops at the edge while keeping its usual MP cost and cooldown.

## World map
- Press M to see the whole world boundary, your position and coordinates.
- With touch controls, tap Map to open the overview, then Close or Back to return.
