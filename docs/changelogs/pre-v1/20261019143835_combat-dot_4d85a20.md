# Burns and poisons stack — 2026-10-19

## Fire
- Land repeated burning hits to keep several burns ticking together, including hits from Fire Missiles.
- Each burn lasts four seconds, dealing damage every half-second. New burns don't replace or prolong older ones.

## Poison
- Repeated dagger special attacks add separate poisons instead of refreshing the first one.
- Each poison ticks once a second for five seconds, keeping the damage of the hit that applied it.

## Fire and poison together
- Burn and poison the same enemy at once. Each application keeps ticking and expires on its own schedule.
