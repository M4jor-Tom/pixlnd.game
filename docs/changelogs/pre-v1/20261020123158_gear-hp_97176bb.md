# Health from equipment — 2026-10-20

## Equipment
- Equipped weapons, chest armor, shoulder armor, gloves and boots now add to your maximum health. Items in your bag do not.
- Otherwise matching pieces give the same health bonus even when their names and rolls differ.
- Swapping equipment never heals you. If your maximum drops below your current health, your health drops to the new cap.

## Recovery
- Leveling up, resting at an inn and respawning refill your health to the maximum that includes your worn equipment.
