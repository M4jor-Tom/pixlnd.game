# Hits finally feel like hits — 2026-09-11

## Hits and kills
- Landing a hit freezes time for an instant and shakes the camera.
- Damage floats up off whatever you hit, in numbers you can read.
- Critical hits show bigger gold numbers.
- Burning and poison ticks float their own orange numbers.
- Kills freeze the screen for a beat and shake the camera hard.
- A flash marks the exact spot where a hit or explosion landed.

## Getting hit
- Getting hit shakes the screen and shows a red number over you.
- Blocking softens the shake and has its own sound.
- Dodging is quiet and smooth, no shake.

## Shooting
- Firing any shot gives a small camera kick.
- Arrows, bolts, and boomerangs leave a fading trail as they fly.
- Shots flash where they hit or land.

## Stuns and buffs
- Stunned enemies (and you) get stars floating over their head until it wears off.
- Active buffs show as little icons with a countdown, not just text.

## Levelling up
- Levelling up pops a bigger "LEVEL UP!" message on screen.

## Pick-ups
- Picking things up and collecting coins now make a sound.

## Sounds
- Every hit, block, shot, and pickup has a sound now, though they're placeholder blips until real sounds arrive.
